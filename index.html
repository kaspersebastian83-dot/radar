Green dot: live quote from the automatic price update. Yellow dot: the last update for that symbol failed, so the previous price is shown. "To buy level" is how far the price has to fall to reach your buy-below. "Total in EUR" is price change plus dividends since you logged the idea (for Bought cards: since the purchase), converted at the exchange rate of that day versus today, before withholding tax.

The Add score works like the Buy score on the Ideas board: 50 points for the distance to your add-below price, 30 for yield vs. its 5-year average, 20 for the position in the 52-week range. Without your own add level, a matching Radar card's buy-below is used; with no level at all a stock can reach "Getting close" but never the add zone. Stocks above your max weight are listed as full, however cheap they get. Share counts and values come from the statement you import and stay in this browser; only the list of holdings and your add levels are saved to GitHub.

How the Buy score works

0 to 100. It only measures how cheap the stock is against your own thesis. Conviction stays separate (the stars).

50 points

Distance to your buy-below price. Full points at or under it, zero at 25 % above.

30 points

Yield vs. its own 5-year average. Full points at 15 % above average, zero at 25 % below.

20 points

Position in the 52-week range. Full points at the low, zero at the high.

75+ Buy zone55–74 Getting close35–54 Waitunder 35 Expensive

If one input is missing (no buy price, no dividend history), its points are spread over the others. Bought positions are scored the same way, so you can see when it is a good moment to add.

Max weight and Add scoreHow "Full" and the score work

The Add score works like the Buy score: 60 points for the distance to your add level, 30 for yield vs. its 5-year average, 10 for the 52-week range. Add zone only at or below your add level. Without an add level (your own, or the buy-below of a Radar card on the same symbol) a stock can't reach the add zone, so it is listed under "No add level yet". Stocks above your max weight are listed as full. The max moves 1.5 points per star of conviction (set in each holding: 5★ = +3, 1★ = −3), unless you give a holding its own max. An unchecked red flag holds a stock at Hold.

How the Buy score works

0 to 100. It only measures how cheap the stock is against your own thesis. Conviction stays separate (the stars).

60 points

Distance to your buy-below price. Full points at or under it, zero at 25 % above.

30 points

Yield vs. its own 5-year average. Full points at 15 % above average, zero at 25 % below.

10 points

Position in the 52-week range. Full points at the low, zero at the high. Kept small on purpose: a stock near its low is often cheap for a reason.

If one input is missing (no buy price, no dividend history), its points are spread over the others. Bought positions are scored the same way, so you can see when it is a good moment to add.

Three limits on top

Red flag in the business: the score stops at 54 (Wait) while the business shows an unchecked red flag: cash per share falling 2 years in a row, dividend not covered for 2 years, a dividend cut, share count up without per-share growth, or debt rising fast. Open the card, look at it, and mark it as checked if there's a harmless reason. The next year's figures raise it again.

Above your price: Buy zone only at or below your buy-below. Even 1 % above, the score stops at 74 (Getting close), however good the yield looks.

No buy price: without your own buy-below, yield and 52-week range alone stop at 74 (Getting close).

How the Buy score works

0 to 100. It only measures how cheap the stock is against your own thesis. Conviction stays separate (the stars).

50 points

Distance to your buy-below price. Full points at or under it, zero at 25 % above.

30 points

Yield vs. its own 5-year average. Full points at 15 % above average, zero at 25 % below.

20 points

Position in the 52-week range. Full points at the low, zero at the high.

75+ Buy zone55–74 Getting close35–54 Waitunder 35 Expensive

If one input is missing (no buy price, no dividend history), its points are spread over the others. Bought positions are scored the same way, so you can see when it is a good moment to add.
